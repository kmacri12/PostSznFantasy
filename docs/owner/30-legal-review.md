# Step 30: Lawyer review of the Privacy Policy and Terms

A call or two, plus the lawyer's turnaround (allow 2 to 4 weeks). Gate: no public store submission until the reviewed pages are live. Needs [Step 25](25-llc.md) and the drafts from [workstream 05](../mobile/workstreams/05-legal.md). Part of the [native apps plan](../mobile/README.md) (not started). [Back to the list](README.md)

1. **Find a lawyer** who does small software or internet businesses: your state bar's referral service, or a recommendation. Ask for a **flat fee** to review two documents for a free app, and say up front it is a small side business.
2. **Send them**:
   - the draft Privacy Policy and Terms (the coordinator exports them from the website pages)
   - the "Who processes it" list from the Privacy Policy and a one-paragraph description of the app
   - the LLC's state filing and operating agreement
   - Tank01's answer ([Step 29](29-rights-check.md))
3. **Ask them**:
   - Is anything missing for a free US-only app with user chat and pictures, available to 13-year-olds and up (COPPA)?
   - Which state's law governs, and what liability limit and disclaimer wording fits?
   - Is the licence to show what members post worded right?
   - Should we use Apple's standard end-user licence or our own Terms in the App Store?
   - Do state privacy laws (California and others) apply at our size, and is anything required anyway?
   - Can the app and website show team names, player names and team logos?
   - Is the "Free game, no money" section enough given that leagues may play for money among themselves?
   - Does the LLC need anything else (insurance, separate agreements) to actually protect me?
4. **Hand the answers to the coordinator**, who applies them to the pages (and the logo switch, if cleared) in a PR and dates them.

## Verify

- The live `/privacy` and `/terms` carry the reviewed text and a new date.
- The lawyer's written notes are saved with the company records.
