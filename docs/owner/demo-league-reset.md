# Reset the reviewers' demo league

5 minutes, after each store review (approved or not). Part of the [native apps plan](../mobile/README.md); the league itself is set up in [Step 31](31-store-submission.md). [Back to the list](README.md)

Each Apple or Google reviewer joins `PostSzn Demo` with their own account and takes its one open team. Before the next review, the team must be open again.

1. Open `PostSzn Demo` as its commissioner, **League settings**, **Members**.
2. Remove every member who is not you or one of the three filler accounts (reviewers appear under their Apple or Google names, often a private relay address). Removing them revokes the invite.
3. **Invite**, **Regenerate**: copy the new code.
4. Paste the new code into the review notes in App Store Connect (App Review Information) and Play Console (App content, App access) before the next submission.
5. If a reviewer posted chat or changed the open team's lineup, leave it: it shows the league is used.

## Verify

The demo league shows one open team, and the invite code in both stores' review notes is the current one.
