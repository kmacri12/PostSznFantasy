# 10: Store submission

Phase D. Needs every other workstream merged and all three [gates](../README.md#gates) closed. The submitting itself is owner step [31](../../owner/31-store-submission.md); this workstream prepares everything it needs. [Back to the plan](../README.md)

## Goal

Both store listings, every questionnaire answer and every image are ready in the repo, so the owner's submission is copying, uploading and pressing Submit.

## Scope

All text lives in `docs/mobile/store/` so it is reviewed in a PR and reused next year.

1. **Listing copy**: name "PostSzn Fantasy", subtitle (Apple, 30 characters) and short description (Play, 80), full description, keywords (Apple). Never use "NFL" or team names as keywords, and repeat "not affiliated with the NFL". Support URL, marketing URL, privacy URL, `/delete-account` URL.
2. **Screenshots**: generated, not drawn, from the same seeded e2e stack the tour uses (`make tour`, `e2e/tour/`): a capture run at the sizes each store requires at the time (Apple's largest iPhone class; Play phone). League, Draft, Team, Feed, NFL schedule, recap. Play also needs a 1024x500 feature graphic.
3. **Apple privacy labels and Play Data safety**: one table in `docs/mobile/store/privacy-answers.md` mapping each data type from the Privacy Policy (05) to the questionnaire answers: collected or not, linked to the user, used for tracking (never), purpose. Includes the Play deletion web link.
4. **Age rating**: Apple's questionnaire and Play's IARC questionnaire, answered for user chat with GIFs (GIPHY at `pg-13`), no gambling or prizes, no ads. Decide here whether lowering GIPHY to `pg` is worth a lower rating.
5. **Other declarations**: encryption (exempt, HTTPS only), content rights (third-party data: Tank01 per step 29; logos only if cleared), ads (none), target audience 13+ (Play; not designed for children), US only.
6. **Review notes**: what the app is, that it is free with no money involved, how to get in ("Sign in with Apple or Google using your own account, then enter invite code XXXX under Your leagues"), what to look at (draft room, team page, Feed with report and block, notifications), and that it is a seasonal game. The demo league is owner step 31.
7. **Smart App Banner**: the `apple-itunes-app` meta tag for `index.html` once the App Store id exists (11 adds it after approval).

## Done when

`docs/mobile/store/` holds the copy, the questionnaire answers and the review notes; screenshots are generated by a script anyone can re-run; the owner has everything owner step 31 lists.

## Re-verify before starting

- Each store's current screenshot sizes, character limits and questionnaires (Apple's age-rating questionnaire changed in 2025).
- Apple guideline 5.2 (intellectual property) and Play's metadata policy on trademarks.
